Lab 5: JDBC transactions with rollback

Prof. Ananya KrishnanPosted 23 Oct20 pointsDue Mon 26 Oct, 11:59 pm

Extend your BankTransfer program so that debiting one account and crediting another happen as a single unit of work. If either update fails, nothing in the database should change.

What to do

  1. Turn off auto-commit on the connection before the two updates.
  2. Commit only after both updates succeed, and roll back in the catch block.
  3. Record every attempt in the transaction_log table with its outcome.
  4. Try a transfer larger than the balance and screenshot the rollback.
